The sales order is not confirmed while something sets it apart from the
customer's form, until it is fixed or a Sales administrator ignores its
exceptions. Each restriction is a rule of the sale exception engine:

- **Payment terms** other than the ones of the customer's form.
- **Delivery method**: an order with goods and no delivery method, or
  with another method than the one of its delivery address or its
  company. A method where the customer collects the goods (a pickup) is
  valid for any address.
- **Customer's purchase order**: a customer marked "Purchase Order
  Required" needs its number in the Customer Reference.
- **Urgent order**, with the "Urgent" tag: a Sales administrator
  confirms it.
- **Quantity with decimals** of goods counted in units (the unit, a
  dozen, a pack), like 1.5 units: nobody ignores it, the line is fixed.
  Setting the precision of the quantities to whole numbers is not the
  same: it rounds without warning.
- **Expired quotation**: it is not confirmed with its old prices.
  "Update Prices", shown only on the expired quotation, brings today's
  prices with the same recalculation as changing the pricelist, today's
  date and a new expiration, and leaves the total before and after in
  the history.
- **Credit limit**, archived: the order that takes the customer over its
  credit limit, as the quotation warns (what the customer owes, its
  confirmed orders to invoice and this order). The company that wants to
  stop it activates the rule.

Besides:

- Only a Sales administrator ignores the exceptions or unlocks a
  confirmed order, also through the API.
- The portal and automated processes are not stopped by any rule: the
  order is confirmed with the differences in its note and an activity
  for its salesperson.
- Confirmed orders are locked for everyone.
- An order with goods delivered or invoiced is not cancelled: it is
  undone with the return and the credit note.

Configuration
=============

- On each customer that works with purchase orders: Sales & Purchase →
  Purchase Order Required (set on the company; its branches and
  addresses follow it).
- On each pickup delivery method: Customer Collects.
- The rules, in Sales → Configuration → Sale Exception Rules: a Sales
  administrator archives the ones not used (a company that sells goods
  counted in units in fractions archives "Quantity with decimals"); a
  Settings administrator makes blocking the ones nobody may ignore, and
  creates new rules with a filter.
- To stop the orders over the credit limit: Accounting → Configuration →
  Settings → Sales Credit Limit, with its Default Credit Limit, and each
  customer's own limit in its form (Credit Limits); then, in Sale
  Exception Rules, the "Archived" filter and "Unarchive" on "Customer
  over its credit limit".

Usage
=====

Confirm the order as usual. When it is stopped, fix what the exception
says, or a Sales administrator confirms it with "Ignore Exceptions".
