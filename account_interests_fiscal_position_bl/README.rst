The late payment interest of the Odoo community (``account_interests``)
makes, each period, a debit note with the interest of what each customer
owes. The note takes its fiscal position, and with it its VAT, from the
customer's form. When the form no longer says how the overdue invoices
were made, the note goes out with another VAT: a customer that buys now
without VAT and still owes an invoice made with VAT gets a note without
VAT, and the other way round. The interest is part of the price of the
sale paid late, so it carries the VAT of that sale.

This module makes the note follow the invoices it charges:

- **The fiscal position of the overdue invoices**: the note takes the
  one of the invoices whose interest it charges (the ones still overdue
  and, with late payment interest, the ones paid late in the period),
  not the one of the customer's form.
- **Several fiscal positions**: when the overdue invoices of a customer
  in the same company have different ones, which normal work does not
  make, the note takes the one of the largest part of the debt.
- **The taxes, as on an invoice**: the taxes of the interest product,
  mapped by that fiscal position. A branch without taxes of its own uses
  the ones of its parent company, as Odoo does on its invoices: without
  this module, the note of a branch goes out without taxes.
- **Nothing else changes**: the amounts, the lines, the journal, the
  document type and the validation stay as the interests module makes
  them.

Configuration
=============

None. The interests are configured as without this module, in Accounting
→ Configuration → Interest Configuration: one rule per company, with its
interest product and the taxes of that product.

Usage
=====

Nothing changes in the daily work: the scheduled action makes the
interest notes as before, now with the VAT of the invoices they charge.
A rule without automatic validation leaves them in draft: check each one
and confirm it.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- One note per customer and company, with one fiscal position. When the
  overdue invoices of a customer in the same company carry different
  VAT, the note takes the fiscal position of the largest part of the
  debt: before confirming it, split its line by VAT with the detail of
  each invoice that the note leaves in its history.
- One VAT rate per note, the one of the interest product: a company that
  sells with two rates splits the line by rate before confirming the
  note, as without this module.
- The amounts, the periods and the document type are the ones of the
  interests module.
