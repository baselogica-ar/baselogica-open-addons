- One note per customer and company, with one fiscal position. When the overdue invoices
  of a customer in the same company carry different VAT, the note takes the fiscal
  position of the largest part of the debt: before confirming it, split its line by VAT
  with the detail of each invoice that the note leaves in its history.
- One VAT rate per note, the one of the interest product: a company that sells with two
  rates splits the line by rate before confirming the note, as without this module.
- The amounts, the periods and the document type are the ones of the interests module.
