- Only sales orders are checked: an invoice created directly in Invoicing is not stopped.
- The prices and discounts typed on the order are not checked against the pricelist; only
  an expired quotation gets today's prices, with "Update Prices".
- The portal and automated processes, like an online payment, are not stopped: the order
  is confirmed and the differences go to its note, with an activity for its salesperson.
- The credit limit rule is off by default: a company turns it on, and it uses the native
  credit limits.
- A delivered or invoiced order is undone with the return and the credit note, by hand,
  before it can be cancelled.
